Topics I explore

  • Escape-room-style learning tied to performance tasks
  • Onboarding that supports connection, belonging, and application
  • AI as a design and prototyping partner, with human judgment in charge
  • Accessibility, cognitive load, and usable learning experiences
  • Making technical and complex content engaging without losing accuracy

What a session feels like

Participants might solve a mini challenge, test a prototype, compare decisions, or reflect on an experience together. The activity is never just a novelty: it makes the underlying design principle visible.

I aim for takeaways people can adapt, not a presentation that stops being useful when the room empties.

Short speaker bio

Pamela Gutman is a learning experience designer, consultant, trainer, and speaker who creates interactive learning for technical and professional audiences. Her work brings together adult learning principles, accessible design, emerging technology, and experiential approaches, including puzzles and gamification, to help people turn knowledge into action.
