Curious by nature.
Intentional by design.

I'm Pamela Gutman, a learning experience designer, learning consultant, trainer, and speaker. I build experiences that help people find their footing with unfamiliar ideas, technology, and places, then actually use what they've learned.

The professional side

Complex ideas, made usable.

My work brings together learning strategy, instructional design, facilitation, and technology. I work with technical and professional audiences where accuracy matters and time is limited, but where learning still needs to feel approachable.

I care about what happens after the session: whether people can recognize the situation, make a sound decision, and feel confident taking the next step.

My academic background includes two master's degrees and doctoral study in adult learning and leadership. Research informs my design, and real learners keep me honest.

My design compass

Four things I rarely compromise on

  • Start with performance. What must people be able to decide or do?
  • Design for real humans. Make the path understandable, accessible, and flexible.
  • Use play with purpose. Keep the challenge in the learning, not the instructions.
  • Iterate in the open. Watch what people actually do, then improve it.

The human side

Yes, I really do love an escape room.

I'm happiest when a story asks me to explore it: science fiction, fantasy, tabletop role-playing games, immersive experiences, clever puzzles, and unexpected discoveries while traveling.

That curiosity doesn't replace sound design. It helps me ask better questions: Can someone discover this instead of being told? Could a collaborative challenge make a concept easier to remember? What would make this feel welcoming rather than intimidating?

I like the moment a learner realizes they can solve something they didn't know how to approach five minutes ago.
