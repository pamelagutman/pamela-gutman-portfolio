When the superheroes can't save the day

Call the Backup League.

A superhero-headquarters micro escape game where the support crew does the saving and every puzzle puts a real decision-making skill to work.

Mission briefing

The heroes are trapped. The systems are failing.

During a containment accident, the Vanguard became trapped in an unstable field. H.E.L.P.E.R., the headquarters assistant, is still operational but malfunctioning. The support crew must restore the remote rescue system before the Vanguard can be released. No one should enter the containment field.

The challenge has three parallel missions. Teams may divide the work, combine their discoveries, and use the resulting constellation fragments to activate the final containment override.

Three missions • Three performance skills

The mechanics serve the learning.

01

Memory Meltdown

Separate dangerous incidents from distracting noise, then prioritize the response.

Prioritize information
02

Gadget Graveyard

Calibrate an emergency generator while balancing multiple operational constraints.

Decide under constraints
03

Academy Confidential

Find hidden evidence, question apparently reassuring signals, and make a safety call.
