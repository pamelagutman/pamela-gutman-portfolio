Behind the build: audience, format, and design challenge

AUDIENCE

Onboarding practitioners and conference participants

FORMAT

Interactive session, mission passport, and guided debrief

DESIGN CHALLENGE

Turning a large, flexible race concept into a repeatable onboarding approach

Session structure

The deck opens with a briefing, moves into the live challenge, then ends with debrief, idea exchange, and takeaways. The passport includes a map, progress tracker, hint tracker, and scratch paper so teams can move through the race in an organized way.

Reusable framework

  • Define the goal
  • Determine the content
  • Design the challenges
  • Handle logistics and testing

Behind the finished work

My role, decisions & evidence

A little more about what I contributed and how I evaluated the design.

My role
Translated experience running organizational Amazing Race activities into a participant-facing conference session with physical materials, a challenge, and a facilitated debrief.
Design decision
Used a mission passport and a short live activity so people experienced the mechanic before considering how to adapt it to onboarding.
What can be claimed
The session demonstrates a design approach and artifacts. Publicly available materials here do not establish a measured improvement in onboarding outcomes from the conference activity itself.

The transferable principle

What another designer can borrow

A physical passport can provide structure, progress, and shared team momentum.
