What the booklet supported

Participants used a passport booklet, progress tracker, and hint tracker to move across locations and complete challenges. The design made the experience feel playful while still being structured enough to manage a large group.

The overall event blended discovery, teamwork, and organizational connection into a single shared experience.

Highlights from the event

  • Passport and route map
  • Progress and hint tracking
  • Team-based puzzle stations
  • A finale that rewarded speed and collaboration

Behind the finished work

My role, decisions & evidence

A little more about what I contributed and how I evaluated the design.

My role
Helped design, coordinate, and adapt campus-based puzzle experiences, including clues, participant materials, logistics, and game-day operations.
Design decision
The participant passport, introduced in the 2024 Labwide race, became a reusable mechanic in later Discovery Program races. The format gave teams a clear route, progress record, and sense of momentum.
Evidence
The original Labwide experience was designed to encourage creative thinking and exposure to campus resources. Staff informally reported discovering places they had not known about; this was anecdotal rather than a controlled outcome.
Adaptation
A location-rich event can become a conference tabletop exercise or an onboarding activity, but the instructions, clue difficulty, and safety plan must fit the new audience.
