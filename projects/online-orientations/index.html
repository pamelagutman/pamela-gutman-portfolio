The problem behind the presentation

Our orientation had been completely in person. When we moved it online, the challenge was not simply delivering the same information over video. New colleagues still needed to find people, understand unfamiliar resources, and build relationships while learning from home.

We spread the experience across roughly a month and used different modes of participation: recorded tours followed by live questions, interactive presentations, breakout discussions, and collaborative scavenger hunts. We also explored a Gather.Town space as another way to help people interact.

What I made and led

  • Led the orientation program committee and the scavenger-hunt creation team.
  • Designed and facilitated online activities and the overall learning experience.
  • Created and delivered the Learning Solutions 2023 presentation.
  • Developed materials using PowerPoint, Adobe Illustrator, Premiere Pro, and interactive tools including Kahoot!, Mentimeter, Storyline, and Gather.Town.

What I observed

Did it feel like an orientation, or a community?

Participants rated the redesigned program as both effective and engaging. In my original portfolio notes, I described new hires meeting through the program and later collaborating on innovation initiatives, including grants. I also observed positive retention during a difficult hiring period.

These are program ratings and practitioner observations, not evidence that the program alone caused retention outcomes.
